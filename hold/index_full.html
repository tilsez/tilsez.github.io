Our standards

Large claims deserve suspicion. So the programme is built so that trust is unnecessary: you can check.

The six commitments

1. Every statement wears a status — theorem, computation, identification, principle, reading, definition — and the status is never overstated.
2. Every number names the script that reproduces it. A result without its script is not established.
3. Every change, finding, erratum and decision is logged, dated, in one running record — 482 addenda to 19 October 2026.
4. Errata are applied to the sources and recorded as prominently as results.
5. Nothing is assumed. The first principle is read off existence; the law is a principle and says so; no identification or reading is a premise of any proof — checked mechanically.
6. Every book chapter and paper is refereed adversarially before release, by a second reader instructed to find fault, and the findings are applied.

Vijay Dhansukhlal Thakkar, founder and designated partner, is an independent researcher in physics and mathematics with decades in industry, working outside academia — stated as fact, not apology. The programme's method — statuses, scripts, the log, adversarial refereeing — exists so that the work can be judged without reference to affiliations.

The research is a transparent human–machine collaboration: the vantage, direction, principles and readings are the author's; verification, drafting and refereeing are machine-assisted; every computational result is independently re-runnable from the published scripts.

Three ways to engage

Challenge the mathematics.

The second edition's Appendix E collects forty-six objections in the critic's strongest form with their replies by label. An objection that survives is logged with credit. Subject line: "Objection: [edition · chapter · label]".

Run an experiment.

The coupling protocol for drive-switched superconductivity and the Ampère bench protocol are available for collaboration; the result is co-authored whichever way it falls.

Read the book.

Both editions are described under The Book; the first is written for a reader with no background at all.
